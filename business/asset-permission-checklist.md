# Draft Asset Permission Checklist

- Client confirms it owns or is authorized to use each asset.
- Client supplies the source/creator/license for third-party material.
- Client confirms supplied floor plans and property details are accurate.
- Client identifies layout, view, finish, and furnishing variations.
- Production must not add unavailable rooms, amenities, views, or structural features.

Draft for a parent/guardian and qualified professional to review before client use.
