# Quality Rubric (100 points)

This rubric is fixed before production. Automatic gates are mandatory; a numeric score cannot override a failed automatic gate. Items requiring human judgment must be labeled **REQUIRES HUMAN VISUAL CONFIRMATION** when evidence is inconclusive.

| Category | Points | Release evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A. Licensing and truthful presentation | 10 | Verified source, creator, license, attribution, fictional-demo disclosure, and no false claims. **Automatic failure** for unclear/conflicting/noncommercial license or false listing/client claim. |
| B. Architectural fidelity | 15 | Same property in all shots; no invented architecture, geometry drift, false scale, or camera collision. Camera collision is an automatic failure. |
| C. Rendering quality | 15 | No missing/magenta/black materials, broken normals, severe noise/fireflies, clipping, or unstable lighting. |
| D. Camera and composition | 10 | Plausible, smooth, restrained movement; controlled verticals; clear spatial orientation and no excessive distortion. |
| E. Editing and pacing | 10 | Strong opening, 15–20 second coherent progression, motivated cuts, and clean resolution. |
| F. Color and finishing | 8 | Matched exposure/white balance, natural materials, controlled highlights/shadows, and no artificial HDR look. |
| G. Audio and typography | 7 | Licensed audio or intentional silence; no clipping; accurate, legible mobile-safe typography. |
| H. Export integrity | 5 | Correct duration, resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate, codec, playback, no black-frame cluster, and no frozen sequence. |
| I. Website implementation | 10 | Responsive, accessible, functional CTAs, honest form behavior, working video, no console errors/overflow/broken links, and metadata. |
| J. Business clarity and conversion | 5 | Clear audience, offer, truthful same-layout explanation, price framing, and next action. |
| K. Documentation and repeatability | 5 | Setup, asset requirements, licensing, scripts, shot config, review history, and final report. |
| **Total** | **100** | **Release requires every automatic gate to pass, no known critical defect, and evidence-backed 100/100.** |
